01 / A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Ideas matter.
Institutions make them real.

I am an Afghan executive and entrepreneur whose professional journey has taken me through public institutions, international education and professional experience abroad, and the practical work of building organizations.

My academic experience in Japan, professional experience in Canada and work in Afghanistan have shaped my interest in how institutions are governed, how organizations grow, and how ideas can be translated into lasting impact.

Today, my work includes healthcare leadership and entrepreneurial initiatives. I remain interested in the broader questions behind that work: sound governance, capable teams, responsible management and sustainable development.
